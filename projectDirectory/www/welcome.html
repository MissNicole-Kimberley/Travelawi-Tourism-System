About TRAVELAWI

TRAVELAWI is an Integrated Tourism, Travel and Accommodation Management Application developed to promote tourism within Malawi. The platform connects travellers with destinations, accommodation providers, tourism experiences and organised group tours through one convenient application.

Whether you're planning a weekend getaway, a family holiday, or an adventurous group trip, TRAVELAWI makes discovering Malawi simple, secure and enjoyable.
